Ringette · stabbing the ring

Spear, don't chop.

When a pass is coming, get your stick tip low and early in the ring's path and let the ring come to you. Three short animations show why. They're slowed down so you can see what happens, and the ring and stick are drawn to real size.

Your room for error

The hole is about 11 cm across. Your stick tip is about 3 cm wide. That leaves roughly 4 cm on each side, shown by the green dashes. It's a small target, so it's easier to have your tip already waiting in the ring's path than to hit it at the last second.

Tip 1 · side view, cut in half

Spear it low

Hold your tip just above the rim, then drop it in. The rim is 2.5–2.9 cm high, so a stick flat on the ice just pushes the ring away. Chopping from above only works if you're perfectly on time; a little late and you hit the rim.

Tip 2 · top view, pass across your body

Tip where it's going

Put your tip ahead of the ring, on its line, and let it slide onto your stick. If you reach for where the ring is right now, it's already gone by the time your stick gets there. Keep skating at the same speed.

Tip 3 · side view, cut in half

Soft hands

Let your stick give a little when the ring hits it. Stopping the ring takes the same push either way. Stiff hands do it all at once, so the ring bounces off. Soft hands spread it out, so it stays on.

Before the pass

  • Take a quick look around while the ring is far away.
  • Turn toward the passer and call for it.
  • Put your stick on the ice as a target.
  • Watch the passer's stick to see where it's going.
  • Then keep your eyes on the ring all the way into your stick.

When chopping is OK

  • The ring is sitting still.
  • The ring is bouncing or rolling; a short chop pins it flat.
  • A scramble near the net with no room to reach.

Ring and stick sizes come from Ringette Canada's rule book: hole 10.7–11.3 cm, rim 2.5–2.9 cm high, stick 2.7–3.5 cm wide. The tips come from Ringette Canada and Finnish federation coaching material, plus catching research from other sports. Tap any animation to replay it.
